Why Off-Chain Routing Wins

Most on-chain aggregators route 60–80% of traffic through the same DEXs.

That creates bottlenecks, public intent, slippage, MEV risk, and 12s+ confirmation delays.

Swappiq avoids all that:

  • ✓ Execution in 500ms–3s
  • ✓ 70–90% lower slippage
  • ✓ No gas fees until confirmed
  • ✓ Optional fallback to on-chain Uniswap-style path

How It Works

Users sign an off-chain trade intent (EIP-712)

Solvers privately return quotes

Dual-signature confirms final execution

Relayer or solver submits the transaction on-chain

If no quote, fallback to on-chain or cancel safely

FAQ

What is Swappiq?

Swappiq is an off-chain-first DEX aggregator protocol that routes trades through private RFQ (Request for Quote) systems, eliminating gas costs and MEV risks before execution.

How is it different from other aggregators?

Unlike traditional aggregators that route on-chain first, Swappiq defaults to off-chain routing, only falling back to on-chain when necessary. This results in faster execution and better prices.

Is it secure?

Yes. Swappiq uses EIP-712 signatures and dual-signature confirmation. Users maintain full custody and can cancel trades before execution.

Why use off-chain routing?

Off-chain routing provides 500ms-3s execution times, 70-90% lower slippage, zero gas until confirmed, and complete MEV protection.

What happens if off-chain fails?

Swappiq automatically falls back to traditional on-chain routing through Uniswap-style paths, or users can safely cancel their trade intent.

What chains does it support?

Currently supporting Ethereum mainnet with L2 and cross-chain support coming soon.
